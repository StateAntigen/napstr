Pick a track, a measure, a style and a contrast. Every canvas is the phone's timeline: 312 × 36 CSS px at 2×, played colour over the first 32%, and a play-head line where the colours meet. Contrast is the knob for how far apart the loud and the quiet parts are drawn. The app now draws density, contrast percentile 20–90, style undulating fill, at 600 bars — which is where this page starts, in the track-discussion purple.
