napstrfy

NAPSTR COMPANION

Your music. Wherever you are.

Napstrfy connects another computer or phone to your running Napstr desktop. It creates a secure tunnel to stream data using Iroh. Napstrfy lets you browse and play the music in your Napstr folder and ask Napstr to download a track while you are away from the computer.

How it works

  1. Open the Napstrfy page in Napstr and create a one-use QR code.
  2. Scan it with Napstrfy, or paste the manual pairing code, to connect over encrypted Iroh.
  3. Napstr continues to handle Nostr discovery and private Tor downloads.
  4. Verified audio already held by Napstr reaches the paired phone over Iroh.

Napstr must remain open and connected while Napstrfy accesses your desktop library. Podcasts are independent and come directly from their public publishers.

Only pair yourself and people you really trust.

A paired phone can browse your indexed music, fetch its audio, and ask your Napstr desktop to download tracks. Treat pairing like giving someone access to your music library. Remove a phone from Napstr’s Napstrfy page as soon as it should no longer connect.

The QR secret expires after five minutes and can be used successfully only once.
